Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data safe?
Yes. Your squad, settings, and stats are stored only on your device. Nothing is uploaded to a server.

How do I restore my purchase?
Open Settings inside the app and tap "Restore Purchases." Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

I found a bug or a bad translation.
Email us at the address above with as much detail as possible (device, language, what happened) and we'll take a look.

Can I request a new pack, mode, or theme?
Absolutely — send us your idea, we love hearing from players.
