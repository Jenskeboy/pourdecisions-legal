🔒 TL;DR — We collect nothing

Pour Decisions does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything stays on your device. No accounts, no servers, no tracking.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the Pour Decisions app ("Application") for Android, developed by SplitStack ("Service Provider"). By using the Application, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

The Application does not collect any personally identifiable information. The following data is stored locally on your device only and never transmitted anywhere:

  • Player names you enter during gameplay
  • Game settings and configuration preferences
  • Game history and squad statistics
  • Theme and language preferences
  • Sound and haptics settings
  • Age verification status (date of birth is not stored, only pass/fail)

This data is stored using your device's local storage and can be fully cleared by uninstalling the Application.

4. Age Restriction

The Application is intended for users 18 years of age or older. The app contains references to alcohol consumption and adult-themed content. An age verification screen is presented on first launch. The Service Provider does not knowingly collect data from or market to anyone under 18.

5. Alcohol & Content Disclaimer

Pour Decisions is a social party game that includes references to alcohol consumption. The Application encourages responsible drinking. The Service Provider does not promote excessive alcohol consumption and is not responsible for the choices users make while playing.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

All locally stored data persists until you uninstall the Application. There is no server-side data to delete. Uninstalling the app permanently removes all associated data from your device.

As a user in the European Union, you have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Since Pour Decisions stores all data exclusively on your device, you can exercise these rights at any time by clearing app data or uninstalling the Application.
